\begin{letter}{Editorial Office\\Computational Materials Science}

\opening{Dear Editor,}

We submit the revised version of our manuscript entitled
``Influence of J2-plasticity on the effective crack resistance of a
simplified porous material in a numerical experiment'' by Alexander
Schl\"uter, Ronjit Medda, and Ralf M\"uller.

We thank you and the reviewers for the careful assessment of our
manuscript. In response to the reviews, we have revised the manuscript
substantially. In particular, the revised manuscript narrows the title
and claims to the investigated numerical experiment, consistently uses
the term effective crack resistance, and discusses more explicitly the
limitations concerning representativity and homogenized fracture
toughness. We have also clarified the numerical methodology and
interpretation of the two- and three-dimensional results.

For clarity, the detailed point-by-point response to all reviewer
comments is supplied as a separate document. We additionally provide a
marked manuscript showing the changes as a separate upload, while the
revised manuscript file itself is supplied in clean form.

We hope that the revised manuscript addresses the concerns raised during
review and is now suitable for consideration for publication.

\closing{Sincerely,}
